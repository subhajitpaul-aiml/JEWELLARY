About Aranghata Sotodal Club

ঐতিহ্য, সংস্কৃতি ও জনসেবায় শতদলের আত্মনিবেদন

Founded with the noble mission of fostering brotherhood, preservation of Bengali folk culture, and self-less societal service, Aranghata Sotodal Club has grown into one of the most revered cultural landmarks in the Ranaghat subdivision of Nadia district.

What started as an intimate neighborhood worship has transformed into a prestigious cultural extravaganza attracting over one lakh devotees and visitors every Kali Puja. Yet, at our heart remains an unyielding devotion to Mother Shyama and an enduring pledge to uplift our rural surroundings.

Under the visionary stewardship of our executive leadership — led by President Ashish Baran Ukil, Secretary Madhav Ghosh, Cashier Apurba Bhattacharya, Vice-Presidents Arjun Sarkar & Swapan Biswas, and Joint Secretaries Sudeb Koley & Sudipta Dey — Sotodal Club continues to flourish with boundless communal unity and seva.

Year-Round Social Responsibilities

Beyond the four days of festive illumination, Sotodal Club actively conducts annual mega blood donation camps, free medical & eye screening clinics, distribution of winter blankets to underprivileged families, and scholarships for meritorious rural students.

Mandap & Concept 2026

Pandal & Pratima Theme

"মাটির টান ও ডাকবিভূতির অন্বেষণ — বাংলার প্রাচীন পোড়ামাটির স্থাপত্য ও মাতৃরূপ"

Concept 2026

Bishnupur Terracotta Sanctuary

বাঁকুড়ার ঐতিহাসিক রাসমঞ্চ ও বিষ্ণুপুরের পোড়ামাটি কারুকার্যের আদলে মণ্ডপসজ্জা

The Creative Vision

This year, Aranghata Sotodal Club pays homage to the immortal terracotta artisans of Bengal. Craftsmen from Bankura and Midnapore are working over 4 months fabricating hundreds of hand-carved clay tiles depicting stories from the Shree Shree Chandi and rural folklore.

Eco-Friendly & Organic Materials

100% natural clay, bamboo armature, jute fibers, and non-toxic herbal water colors.

Traditional Daker Saaj & Shola Ornamentation

Exquisite silver foil & natural sholapith embellishments crafted by master artisans.

Divine Idol Artistry

চিরাচরিত অভয়রূপা মাতৃমূর্তি

Sculpted by renowned Kumartuli artists, the idol stands 14 feet tall, radiating benevolent calm and fierce divine power with classical large almond eyes and traditional embellishments.

Chandannagar Illumination

চন্দননগরের বর্ণাঢ্য আলোকসজ্জা

Over 2 kilometers of synchronized LED arrays and animated light gates transforming the entire Aranghata station road into a shimmering sea of mythological motifs.

Cultural & Musical Nights

সঙ্গীত ও নাট্য সন্ধ্যার আসর

Four evenings of traditional Baul songs, Shyama Sangeet recitals by distinguished Bengal vocalists, folk dance performances, and one-act theatrical dramas.

How to Reach

Location & Visitor Guide

অরংঘাটা শতদল ক্লাব প্রাঙ্গণে পৌঁছনোর বিস্তারিত পথনির্দেশ

By Local Train (Sealdah - Gede Line)

Get down at Aranghata Railway Station (Eastern Railway). The puja pandal is situated just 400 meters east of Platform No. 1 (a 5-minute easy walking distance or battery-rickshaw/Toto ride).

Direct trains from Sealdah, Ranaghat, and Krishnanagar

By Bus & Road Route (NH-34 / NH-12)

From Ranaghat Junction Bus Stand (approx. 9 km), board regular local buses or auto-rickshaws towards Aranghata Bazar. Auto/Toto stands operate 24x7 throughout the festive puja days.

Designated Free Parking & Safety

Designated two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking available near Aranghata High School Ground (200m from pandal). 24/7 CCTV surveillance and volunteers on duty.
